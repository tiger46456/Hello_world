5th Grade Math Worksheet #5

Division Word Problems

Instructions: Solve each division problem. Show your work and include remainders if needed.

Word Problems

1.
A teacher has 288 stickers to divide equally among 12 students. How many stickers will each student receive?
2.
A charity collected 1,575 cans of food. If they pack 25 cans in each box, how many boxes can they fill?
3.
A rope is 456 inches long. If it is cut into 8 equal pieces, how long will each piece be?
4.
There are 2,184 students going on a field trip. Each bus can hold 42 students. How many buses are needed?
5.
A factory produces 3,600 toys in 15 days. How many toys does it produce per day?
6.
Sarah earned $720 babysitting over the summer. If she worked 36 hours, how much did she earn per hour?
7.
A bookshelf has 875 books organized into 25 equal sections. How many books are in each section?
8.
If 1,344 apples are packed into bags with 16 apples each, how many bags will be filled?
9.
A distance of 2,520 miles is covered in 18 hours. What is the average speed per hour?
10.
A pizza restaurant uses 945 ounces of cheese in 21 days. How many ounces do they use per day?

End of Worksheet #5
