5th Grade Math Worksheet #9

Perimeter, Area, and Geometry

Instructions: Calculate perimeter and area for each shape. Include proper units in your answers.

Part A: Perimeter

1.
Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 12 cm and width 8 cm.
2.
Find the perimeter of a square with sides of 15 inches.
3.
A triangle has sides measuring 7 m, 9 m, and 11 m. What is its perimeter?
4.
Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 25 ft and width 18 ft.

Part B: Area

5.
Find the area of a rectangle with length 14 m and width 9 m.
6.
Find the area of a square with sides of 13 inches.
7.
A rectangular garden is 20 feet long and 12 feet wide. What is its area?
8.
Find the area of a triangle with base 16 cm and height 10 cm. (Area = 1/2 × base × height)

Part C: Combined Problems

9.
A rectangular room has a length of 18 feet and width of 15 feet. How much carpet (area) is needed to cover the floor?
10.
A square playground has sides of 40 meters. What is the perimeter of the playground?

End of Worksheet #9
