5th Grade Math Worksheet #4

Multiplication Word Problems

Instructions: Read each problem carefully. Show your work and write your answer with the correct units.

Word Problems

1.
A box contains 24 pencils. If a teacher orders 15 boxes, how many pencils will she have in total?
2.
Each classroom has 28 desks. If there are 12 classrooms in the school, how many desks are there in total?
3.
A bakery makes 135 cookies every hour. How many cookies will they make in 8 hours?
4.
A train travels 87 miles per hour. How far will it travel in 6 hours if it maintains the same speed?
5.
Maria reads 45 pages of her book each day. How many pages will she read in 30 days?
6.
A movie theater has 18 rows with 32 seats in each row. What is the total seating capacity?
7.
Tom earns $25 per hour. If he works 40 hours in a week, how much will he earn?
8.
A garden has 16 rows of tomato plants with 23 plants in each row. How many tomato plants are there in total?
9.
A pack of markers costs $12. How much would 25 packs cost?
10.
If one package weighs 48 ounces, what is the total weight of 75 packages?

End of Worksheet #4
